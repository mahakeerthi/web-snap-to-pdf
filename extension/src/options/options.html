About

PageSnap PDF renders pages with Chrome's built-in print engine, entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Chrome shows a “PageSnap PDF started debugging this browser” bar while a PDF is being generated. That is how Chrome exposes its PDF engine to extensions; it disappears as soon as the PDF is ready.
