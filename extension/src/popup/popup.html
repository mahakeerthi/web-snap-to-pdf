How it works

  • Full page saves everything on the page as a PDF, with working links.
  • Article only keeps the main text and images and drops menus, sidebars and ads.
  • Select element lets you click one part of the page and export just that part.
  • Remove elements lets you click anything you don’t want before saving. Reset page brings it back.
  • The eye button opens a preview tab instead of downloading.
  • Paper, margins and other options are under Export settings. The line above it always shows what will be used.

Everything runs on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
